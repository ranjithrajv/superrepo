SuperRepo

Every setting lives in this browser. No account, no network calls, no analytics.

Buttons and services

Everything about the buttons in the repo header — what each one is for, which categories get a button, which services appear at all, where they sit and what they look like — is set from the panel itself. Open any repo page, click the More button, and use Settings at the foot of the panel.

Advanced — other sites, your own services, reset

Other sites

Buttons appear on github.com out of the box. Adding another site asks your browser for access to it — SuperRepo only ever reads that page to draw its own buttons in it, and removing the access takes the buttons away again.
